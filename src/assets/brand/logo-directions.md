# Logo Directions

## Wordmark

- Text: **Equi**
- Style: clean geometric sans-serif, medium weight, generous letter spacing optional
- Color: primary blue `#2563eb` on light backgrounds; white on dark hero only if needed

## Icon Ideas

Explore one of these directions (not all at once):

1. **Abstract E** — minimal letterform, balanced strokes
2. **Upward line** — subtle growth trajectory, not aggressive
3. **Portfolio pulse** — gentle waveform or heartbeat suggesting monitoring
4. **Balanced scale / equity** — symmetry suggesting balance and fairness

## Style

- Minimal; no gradients in the primary mark
- Works on white and `#f8fafc` app background
- Legible at **24px** height (favicon, sidebar collapsed)

## Avoid

- Crypto-like logos (coins, rockets, neon gradients)
- Aggressive trading symbols (bulls, bears, tickers as the mark)
- Overly complex chart imagery inside the icon
- Stock-ticker clichés as the primary identity
