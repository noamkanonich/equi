# Product Voice

How Equi speaks in UI copy, AI insights, and empty states.

## Tone

- **Calm** — no urgency or pressure to trade
- **Smart** — informed, structured, explainable
- **Practical** — short sentences, actionable monitoring points
- **Trustworthy** — honest about limits and data gaps

## We Are Not

- Hypey ("moonshot", "can't miss")
- Aggressive ("act now", "you must buy")
- Promising returns or certainty
- Mimicking a trading terminal or meme-stock culture

## Copy Patterns

- Lead with what the data shows, then what it **may** suggest
- Use "Suggested action" not "You should"
- Acknowledge missing or stale data plainly
- Disclaimers where AI or scores inform the message

## Examples

| Instead of | Prefer |
|------------|--------|
| "Buy now before it's too late" | "Suggested action: Watch — consider reviewing valuation" |
| "This stock will soar" | "Momentum scores are strong; worth monitoring" |
| "Guaranteed winner" | "Based on current score, fundamentals look solid with some risks below" |

## Locales

- Maintain the same tone in English and Hebrew translations
- Avoid idioms that do not translate cleanly
