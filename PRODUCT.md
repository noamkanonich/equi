# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Equi is for individual investors who want a clearer way to review holdings, watchlists, risks, and portfolio changes before making their own decisions. Users may check the product during routine portfolio review, after market moves, or before deciding what deserves closer attention.

## Product Purpose

Equi is a personal AI portfolio copilot. It organizes portfolio data, calculates explainable scores, highlights changes, surfaces risks, and suggests calm decision-support actions. Success means users can understand what changed, why it matters, and what may be worth reviewing without the product pretending to trade for them or guarantee outcomes.

## Brand Personality

Calm, smart, practical, and trustworthy. The product should feel clear, premium, and focused rather than loud, gamified, or trading-terminal intense.

## Anti-references

Equi should not look or sound like a hype-driven trading app, meme-stock tool, aggressive brokerage prompt, or dense terminal interface. It should avoid urgency, exaggerated upside claims, noisy motion, cluttered dashboards, and advice language that sounds guaranteed.

## Design Principles

- Make interpretation clear: separate facts, scores, risks, and suggested actions.
- Keep decision support calm: use practical wording and avoid pressure.
- Design for scanning: make portfolio status, changes, and risk signals easy to compare.
- Preserve trust: show confidence, data limits, and disclaimers where AI or scores are involved.
- Support both English and Hebrew from the start, with RTL/LTR layouts treated as first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and both LTR and RTL reading directions. Hebrew and English experiences should have the same information quality and tone.
